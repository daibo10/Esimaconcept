About ESIMA CONCEPT ENTERPRISE

Who we are

ESIMA CONCEPT ENTERPRISE (BN: 9798137) is a forward-thinking sanitation and water solutions company based in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State. We provide modern, eco-friendly alternatives to traditional septic systems, delivering clean, durable and cost-effective solutions for homes, estates and businesses across Nigeria — eliminating odour, preventing water pollution, and ensuring a healthier environment.

We specialize in the design and installation of Biodigester Systems, Chamberless Clean-Out Systems, professional plumbing, and complete water treatment systems. Unlike conventional septic tanks, our systems are non-polluting, odourless, require no evacuation, and are built to last. Whether it's a single bungalow, a duplex, an estate, or a commercial facility, we deliver efficient solutions tailored to your site and soil condition — with quality workmanship, affordable pricing, and excellent after-service support.

Our services

Four systems. One goal — a clean, durable property.

Every installation is matched to your soil condition and site, whether it's a home, an estate or a commercial facility.

A

Biodigester System

A modern sewage treatment system that replaces the traditional septic tank. No odour, no evacuation, eco-friendly, saves space and lasts longer — ideal for all soil types, including waterlogged areas like Delta, Bayelsa, Rivers and other parts of Nigeria.

  • No odour or evacuation needed
  • Compact footprint, long lifespan
  • Performs in waterlogged soil
B

Chamberless Clean-Out System

An advanced drainage inspection and maintenance system that does away with bulky concrete chambers — preventing blockages, easier to maintain, and finished neatly against the building.

  • Prevents blockages before they start
  • Simple to inspect and maintain
  • Clean, discreet finishing
C

Professional Plumbing Services

Complete plumbing for new buildings and maintenance for existing properties — pipe laying, sanitary fittings, water supply and drainage, all installed neatly and built to last.

  • New-build and retrofit plumbing
  • Water supply and drainage lines
  • Sanitary fittings and repairs
D

Complete Water Treatment System

We treat borehole water to make it clean, odour-free and safe for domestic and industrial use — handling iron removal, purification, softening and filtration. We also set up factories for sachet and bottle water.

  • Iron removal & purification
  • Water softening & filtration
  • Sachet & bottle water factory setup
