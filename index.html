Our Services

Four connected services for a clean, safe and lasting property.

Biodigester System

A modern sewage treatment system that replaces septic tanks. No odour, no evacuation, eco-friendly, saves space and lasts longer. Ideal for all soil types, including waterlogged areas in Delta, Bayelsa, Rivers and all parts of Nigeria.

Chamberless Clean-Out System

Advanced drainage inspection and maintenance without concrete chambers. It prevents blockages, is easier to maintain and gives a neat finish.

Professional Plumbing Services

Complete plumbing for new buildings and maintenance for existing properties. Pipe laying, sanitary fittings, water supply and drainage, neatly and durably installed.

Complete Water Treatment System

We treat borehole water to make it clean, odour-free and safe for domestic and industrial use: iron removal, purification, softening and filtration. We also set up factories for sachet and bottle water.
