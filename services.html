Biodigester Systems – Our Core Expertise

The modern, permanent solution to sewage treatment

What is a Biodigester?

A biodigester is a sealed, airtight concrete or modular tank that uses naturally occurring anaerobic bacteria to break down human and organic waste completely – without oxygen.

Unlike a traditional septic tank that simply stores waste until it is full and must be emptied by a tanker, a biodigester continuously digests the solid matter. The end products are:

  • Clear, pathogen-reduced water that can be safely discharged into a soakaway or reused for non-potable purposes
  • Small amounts of biogas (methane + CO₂)
  • Minimal residual sludge that does not accumulate to fill the tank

How an ESIMA Biodigester Works – Step by Step

1. Collection

Black water (toilet waste) and, where designed, grey water enter the biodigester through an inlet pipe. A grease trap can be added upstream for kitchen lines.

2. Anaerobic Digestion

Inside the sealed chamber, specialised bacteria break down complex organic matter through hydrolysis, acidogenesis, acetogenesis and methanogenesis – converting solids into water, gases and a nutrient-rich liquid.

3. Separation & Discharge

Clarified liquid exits through an outlet into a soakaway, French drain or secondary treatment. Solids are continuously digested so the tank never fills up.

4. Bio-Media Enhancement

We install high-quality bio-media (such as coconut fibre or specialised plastic media) to maximise bacterial surface area and accelerate the digestion process.

Key Advantages of ESIMA Biodigesters

  • Never fills up – no more expensive and messy tanker services for the life of the system
  • Odour-free – the sealed design and complete digestion eliminate foul smells
  • Space-efficient – smaller footprint than conventional septic + soakaway combinations
  • Environmentally safe – protects groundwater and reduces pollution
  • Low maintenance – periodic inspection only; no regular emptying
  • Suitable for all scales – from single homes to hotels, schools, hospitals and housing estates
  • Climate-smart – contributes to reduced greenhouse-gas emissions compared with open dumping or poorly managed septic systems

Our trained technicians carefully place bio-media to ensure maximum bacterial activity.

Clean-Out Systems

Professional inspection, evacuation and rehabilitation

Even the best systems eventually need attention. ESIMA provides complete clean-out services for existing septic tanks, soakaways, inspection chambers and drainage lines.

  • Safe mechanical and manual evacuation
  • High-pressure jetting of blocked lines
  • CCTV or visual inspection where required
  • Repair or conversion of old septic tanks into biodigesters
  • Proper disposal of waste in accordance with environmental guidelines

Whether you have an emergency overflow or want a scheduled maintenance clean, our team responds promptly across Yenagoa and surrounding areas.
