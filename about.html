About ESIMA CONCEPT ENTERPRISE

Building smarter, cleaner communities across Nigeria and Africa

Company Profile

ESIMA CONCEPT ENTERPRISE (BN: 9798137) is a registered Nigerian company based in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State. We specialise in the design, construction and maintenance of modern biodigester systems, professional clean-out services and comprehensive water treatment solutions.

Headquartered at 64 Pere Esimein Street, Off Sunny Blue, Azikoro Town, Yenagoa, we serve residential, commercial, institutional and industrial clients across the Niger Delta and beyond. Our team combines local knowledge with proven engineering practices to deliver durable, environmentally responsible sanitation infrastructure.

Our slogan – “Build Smart! Live Better!” – captures our commitment to intelligent, long-lasting solutions that improve the quality of life for families, businesses and communities.

Our Vision

To become the leading provider of sustainable biodigester and water treatment solutions in Nigeria and a recognised name across Africa – enabling every household, estate and institution to enjoy clean, odour-free and environmentally safe sanitation systems that never require costly emptying.

Our Mission

To design, install and maintain high-quality biodigester systems, clean-out solutions and water treatment plants that protect public health, conserve water resources and support Nigeria’s drive toward improved sanitation and climate-smart infrastructure.

Our Policies

  • Quality First: Every installation follows strict engineering standards and uses durable materials suited to Nigerian soil and climate conditions.
  • Environmental Responsibility: We prioritise systems that eliminate the need for frequent tankering, reduce groundwater pollution and produce reusable by-products.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Transparent quotations, clear timelines and after-sales support are standard on every project.
  • Health & Safety: All site works comply with Nigerian safety regulations; our technicians use appropriate personal protective equipment.
  • Integrity: We deliver what we promise – no hidden costs, no substandard workmanship.

Our Ambition for Nigeria & Africa

Scaling clean sanitation technology across the continent

Nigerian Market

We aim to make modern biodigesters the preferred alternative to traditional septic tanks in every state. By partnering with developers, local governments and homeowners, we will reduce the public-health burden of overflowing septic systems and support Nigeria’s sanitation goals under the SDGs.

African Expansion

Beyond Nigeria, ESIMA seeks strategic partnerships to introduce proven biodigester technology in West and Central African markets where rapid urbanisation is creating urgent demand for decentralised, low-maintenance sanitation solutions.

Capacity Building

We are committed to training local artisans and technicians so that quality biodigester construction becomes a transferable skill that strengthens communities and creates green jobs across the region.

Work With a Team That Cares

From single-family homes to large estates and commercial facilities, ESIMA CONCEPT ENTERPRISE delivers reliable sanitation and water solutions backed by professional service.
